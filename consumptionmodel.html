Interactive worlding experience created to encourage players to think and assess the current technology landscape and digital oligarchy. Revolves around Feenburg’s 2012 paper Re)Inventing The Internet : Critical Case Studies discussing the different models of the internet, in particular the consumption model, and themes of digital oligarchy, trans-humanism and cyborgs (Haraway, 1985). A world where these themes where these themes manifest is created.

The consumption model of the internet: "This is the final extension of capitalist rationalisation into every corner of our lives," Feenburg, 2012.

The digital world itself acts as a mechanism of the world’s oligarchy, as an embodiment of the internet consumption model. It is designed to make people question their relationship with the internet and the interrelationship between digital technology and society, displaying the dangers of power embedded within technology whilst also highlighting the positives of the community model of the internet and collective organisation.

Solo project released on 8th June 2025
