Interactive, immersive experience commissioned by Waterside Arts for Prism 2025, 20-22nd November 2025.

Installation inspired by solar punk, science fiction and optimistic visions of a sustainable future. Using the video game technology Unity, the speculative world reacts to the collective decisions of audience members, who interact through a website on their phones.

Solar-punk: an anti-dystopian, anti-capitalist aesthetic that looks practically at creating a sustainable future, optimistically imagining a world where we have overcome climate change. It’s not about ignoring the problem, but instead thinking about solutions and ways that we can coexist with the planet and each other, and encouraging hope in this age of climate anxiety.

Immerse yourself in and connect with this space of optimism
