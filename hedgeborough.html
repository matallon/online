An open world game about the city of Hedgeborough, where a newspaper about declining hedgehog numbers kickstarts community action to improve the biodiversity of the urban area, helping to bring the city together to improve public spaces and maybe bring back hedgehogs too....

Revolves around re-thinking urban environments and using digital tools to engage young people with urban community, nature and wildlife conservation. Looks at digital natives’ relationships with technology and how their reliance on digital networks can mean that they are less aware of local community and their own physical environment. To reach this generation on their own terms and using technology familiar to them, the digital game Hedgeborough encourages players to consider their own agency and the potential and limitations of their actions to rewild local space and positively impact their environment. It aims to demonstrate to players the potential of future sustainable urban environments, encouraging them to engage in the community and physical environment around them through engaging them in a playful, open-world game experience.
