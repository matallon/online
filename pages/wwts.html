we weather the storms

As part of arebyte Digital Art Centre’s annual artist development programme 'Hotel Generation', I developed a proposal for a new exhibition for the arebyte gallery space. My proposal is called 'we weather the storms'. It is a multiplayer game experience where audiences connect through their phones to a single living virtual world, collectively responding to an unpredictable weather system within a solarpunk-inspired ecosystem. ⁠Players are randomly assigned different controls, meaning the only way to recover the simulated environment faster is by working together. Reclaiming the phone from a tool of corporate control into one of connection, the exhibition runs on a largely solar-powered server, built to spark eco hope and collective responsibility, not just for fellow players in the room, but with strangers who shaped the world before they arrived.⁠⁠ Whilst the environment is inspired by solarpunk aesthetics and ideas of sustainable futures, it moves away from science fiction, instead thinking about how we achieve this ‘utopian’ future with what we already have now, and what is restricting us from accessing this future now. It’s about highlighting the capitalist, colonial systems that need dismantling in order for us everyone to access this future and fighting for a more equitable tech landscape.
