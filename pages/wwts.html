My proposal is called 'we weather the storms'. It is a multiplayer game experience where audiences connect through their phones to a single living virtual world, collectively responding to an unpredictable weather system within a solarpunk-inspired ecosystem. ⁠Players are randomly assigned different controls, meaning the only way to recover the simulated environment faster is by working together. Reclaiming the phone from a tool of corporate control into one of connection, the exhibition runs on a largely solar-powered server, built to spark eco hope and collective responsibility, not just for fellow players in the room, but with strangers who shaped the world before they arrived.⁠⁠

During this artist development programme, I was mentored by Sam Mercer, Curator of Digital at The Photographers' Gallery

Video of me discussing my proposal

This proposal is not currently being realised anywhere, however I would love to make this a reality and expand upon ideas explored in this proposal to exist within a different space or scenario.
