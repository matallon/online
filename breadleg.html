Breadleg is an absurdist, comedy game about running round a scottish village as a piece of bread with legs. Encounter the people of the village and collect socks to help you escape without being eaten, whilst maybe getting involved in a bit of mischief along the way.

Solo project. Released on itch.io on 17th May 2025, but I'm open to develop this further!
