Digital worldbuilder, creative technologist and game artist from Leeds creating digital games and immersive artworks, with a socially engaged practice. Largely interested in the potential of digital experiences to impact how people can view their physical environment around them, particularly in environmental and educational contexts, whilst leaning into game art and glitch aesthetics.
I’ve recently completed my MSc in Digital Media and Design at Edinburgh College of Art, and in 2023 graduated with a first from Goldsmiths University in BSc Digital Arts Computing. I primarily work in the game engine Unity and 3D model in Blender, creating works for PC, Mac and VR/XR.
